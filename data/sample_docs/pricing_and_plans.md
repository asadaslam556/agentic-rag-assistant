# Pricing and plans

Auralis Dynamics sells robot capacity through three plans. All plans include
the Atlas Hive fleet software. The plans differ in scale, support level, and
analytics depth.

## Pilot plan

The Pilot plan costs EUR 0 for a 30-day evaluation with up to 2 robots at one
site. It includes remote onboarding and standard business-hours support. Pilot
deployments convert to Growth or Scale at the end of the evaluation.

## Growth plan

The Growth plan costs EUR 449 per robot per month. It targets single-site
fleets of up to 25 robots and includes business-hours support, quarterly
service visits, and core Hive analytics.

## Scale plan

Scale plan cost: EUR 649 per robot per month, billed annually. Scale is built
for multi-site fleets and includes 24/7 support, a 99.5 percent uptime service
level agreement, advanced Hive analytics, and a named technical account
manager.

## Hardware and fees

Robots can be purchased outright or leased. The Atlas P2 list price is
EUR 38,500 per robot. Hardware lease for the Atlas P2 is EUR 1,190 per month
per robot with a 24-month minimum term. A one-time onboarding fee of EUR 4,900
per site applies. The onboarding fee is waived for annual Scale plan
commitments.
