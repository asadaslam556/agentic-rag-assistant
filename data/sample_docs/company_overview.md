# Auralis Dynamics company overview

Auralis Dynamics was founded in 2019 in Munich, Germany. The company builds
autonomous mobile robots (AMRs) and fleet software for warehouse and intralogistics
operations. Auralis Dynamics employs around 240 people across three locations.

## Offices and reach

The headquarters and main production line are in Munich. A software engineering
office operates in Rotterdam, and a sales and support hub covers North America
from Austin, Texas. Auralis has deployed 1,850 robots across 92 customer sites
in 14 countries.

## Funding and growth

Auralis Dynamics closed a Series B round of EUR 45 million in March 2024, led by
Lakeshore Industrial Ventures. The round funds production capacity in Munich and
the expansion of the Atlas Hive software platform. Revenue comes from hardware
sales, hardware leasing, and per-robot software subscriptions.

## Mission

The company mission is to make warehouse automation practical for mid-sized
operators, not only for the largest logistics groups. Deployments are designed
to run alongside existing racking, forklifts, and people rather than requiring
a rebuilt facility.
