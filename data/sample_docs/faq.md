# Frequently asked questions

## How long does a deployment take?

A typical Atlas deployment takes 2 to 6 weeks from site survey to go-live.
Single-zone pilots sit at the short end of that range. Multi-zone sites with
WMS integration usually need the full 6 weeks.

## Which systems does Atlas Hive integrate with?

Atlas Hive integrates over a REST API and webhooks. Certified connectors exist
for SAP EWM and Manhattan Active WM. Custom middleware integrations are
supported through the same public API used by the certified connectors.

## What happens when a robot fails?

Failed jobs are reassigned to the nearest available robot automatically.
Hardware faults raise an alert in Hive and, on the Scale plan, open a ticket
with the 24/7 support team. Every Atlas P2 ships with a 24-month hardware
warranty covering parts and remote diagnostics.

## Can Atlas robots work in cold storage?

The standard Atlas P2 operates between 5 and 40 degrees Celsius. A cold-chain
variant rated down to minus 25 degrees Celsius is available on request with a
modified battery pack and heated sensor housings.

## Do I need new floor infrastructure?

No floor infrastructure is required. Navigation relies on lidar and visual
SLAM, so there are no magnetic strips, QR grids, or reflectors to install or
maintain.
