# Safety and compliance

Safety certification comes first in every Auralis deployment, because Atlas
robots share aisles with people, forklifts, and manual pallet trucks.

## Standards

The Atlas platform is certified to the ISO 3691-4:2023 safety standard for
driverless industrial trucks. Every robot carries the CE marking for the
European market. The safety concept follows the protective field approach of
ISO 3691-4: layered lidar zones slow and then stop the robot before contact
can occur.

## On-robot safety features

Each Atlas P2 has two certified safety lidars covering 360 degrees, a physical
emergency stop button on each side, and audible plus visual movement warnings.
Protective fields scale with speed and load. When a field is breached the robot
performs a controlled stop within the certified stopping distance.

## Data protection and cloud security

Atlas Hive telemetry is processed in the European Union and handled under GDPR.
Camera streams used for navigation are processed on the robot, and no raw video
leaves the vehicle by default. The Hive cloud platform holds a SOC 2 Type II
attestation, renewed annually.
